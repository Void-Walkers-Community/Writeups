Mission Control--> jctf{apollo_11}

Honestly, this one didn't require much heavy OSINT work the moment I read "early space race" and "mission control" together, one mission immediately came to mind Apollo 11.
Think about it. When someone says mission control, the image that pops into everyone's head is that famous room full of controllers in white shirts and ties, erupting in celebration as Apollo 11 successfully landed on the Moon it's basically the poster child for what mission control even is.
I considered Apollo 13 for a second since that's another iconic mission control moment, but that mission is remembered for a crisis rather than a space race achievement the challenge description felt more like it was pointing to a milestone.
So I went with Apollo 11 and it was correct.
