We Have 图寻 at Home challenge - 


The challenge provides an image of an office park in Shenzhen, China. Using the Baidu Apollo autonomous car visible in the foreground as a landmark, the location was identified as the Taixinglong Industrial Park in the Baoan District of Shenzhen.

Geolocating the specific building on the right leads to the headquarters of XTX Technology (芯天下), a semiconductor company specializing in flash memory.

According to the challenge description, the required chip is a Serial NOR Flash with 1024 KB (8M-bit) capacity and a clock frequency of 108MHz. By navigating to the XTX Technology official website and checking their Serial NOR Flash product catalog under the 8M-bit category, the chip matching these specific technical parameters is listed as XT25F08B-S.
https://en.chipsourcetek.com/Other-Chip/2533.html
