Hidden In Plain Sight
The challenge gave me an image of a numbered 9×9 grid. The description said the evidence might be misleading and that a word was important.

I checked the image’s metadata and found a clue:
“Don't Jump when their is the red, otherwise you should!”

The grid could be divided into nine smaller 3×3 sections. Each section had numbers that could be followed in order. I traced them, treating the jumps as breaks and using the red marks to identify the diagonal connections that should remain.
