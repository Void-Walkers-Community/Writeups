Challenge has provided with a png file "Space_Man.png" I did the usual recon : file, exiftool, binwalk but nothing found the I ran zsteg and found "pgfn{jm_ilawfm_zs_sw_gw_zlq_ubwt}" There it is , clearly a flag but encrpyted 

reading the description "The key is one of the most important space projects that paved the way for us to get to the Moon" The big American space programs leading up to the Moon landing were Mercury-->Gemini-->Apollo. Mercury was first, Apollo was the Moon landing itself, so Gemini is the one that "paved the way." "One of their missions was pretty scary. Thank goodness the crew knew how to handle the dizzying situation."
Gemini 8 (March 1966) Neil Armstrong and David Scott's mission, went into a violent, uncontrolled spin due to a stuck thruster firing continuously. The spacecraft was rotating once per second. Armstrong managed to stabilize it by cutting the Orbital Maneuvering System and firing the reentry thrusters, but it forced an emergency splashdown. 
so the key is "gemini"

vignere decrypt with key "gemini" gave the flag:  jctf{we_choose_to_go_to_the_moon}
