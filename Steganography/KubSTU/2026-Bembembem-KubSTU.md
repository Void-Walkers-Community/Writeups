### **Challenge Solution: "bembembem"**

1.  **Locate the File:** There is a **ZIP file** embedded or hidden within the video.
2.  **Find the Hint:** Look for a **text file** provided with the challenge; it contains hints about where the specific data is located.
3.  **Analyze the Audio:** * Go to the **42nd minute** of the audio/video.
    * Perform a **Spectrogram analysis** on the audio at that timestamp.
4.  **Get the Password:** The password for the ZIP file is hidden within that spectrogram.
5.  **Extract & Finish:** * Use the password to extract the ZIP file.
    * Inside, you will find `flag.txt`, which contains the final answer.

---

**Summary of steps:**
`Video` → `ZIP File` → `Audio Spectrogram (at 42:00)` → `ZIP Password` → `flag.txt`
